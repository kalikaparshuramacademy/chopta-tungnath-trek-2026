# Section 2 — 10 Reel / Short-Form Video Ideas

Format: 9:16, 15–30 sec, hook in first 1.5 sec, burned-in captions, trending audio.

### Reel 1 — "₹5,499 vs ₹12,000" price reveal

3 cuts: heavy breathing climb → final step → 360° pan reveal. Audio: emotional drop track. Text: "Worth every step. ₹5,499."

Build a recurring AI host (female Garhwali character) using HeyGen / D-ID. One scheme per reel:

- 3D: "Why June is the *only* good month for Tungnath"

8–10 reels with the **same character** = brand recall + cheap production.

### Reel 4 — "Things parents will ask + our answers"
Rapid Q&A on B-roll: "Is it safe?" → "0 evacuations." "Female safety?" → "Female leader every batch." 8 questions in 25s.

### Reel 5 — "Day 3 in 30 seconds" timelapse
Sunrise → breakfast → trek → temple → summit → descent. Trending Bollywood "yatra" audio. End: "This is Day 3 of 5."

### Reel 6 — "What you pack vs what you use" meme
Side-by-side: huge bag → 4 things actually used (water, fleece, headlamp, snacks). Trending "expectation vs reality" audio.

### Reel 7 — "Solo traveller batch reveal"
Tinder-style swipe through AI avatars: "Riya, Delhi, Engineer" → "Karan, Bangalore, Designer" → "you?" CTA: "Your batch is forming. June 21."

### Reel 8 — Testimonial reaction
Founder reads a real WhatsApp testimonial out loud, gets emotional. Authentic > polished. Caption: "We saved this DM."

### Reel 9 — "Hidden cost calculator"
Text on screen: competitor "₹4,999" → +permits ₹500 → +meals ₹800 → +pickup ₹600 → "Real cost ₹6,900." Cut: "Peak & River: ₹5,499. All-in."

### Reel 10 — Countdown reels (auto-weekly)
Day 30 → Day 14 → Day 7 → Day 1. Same format, same audio. By Day 1 the algorithm knows the format.
