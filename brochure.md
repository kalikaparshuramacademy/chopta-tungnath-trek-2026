### Medical & Emergency Infrastructure

1. **Medical Grade First Aid:** Our trek leaders carry comprehensive high-altitude medical kits, including Oximeters, Oxygen Canisters, and blood pressure monitors.
2. **Evacuation Readiness:** Pre-mapped evacuation routes to the nearest base hospital in Ukhimath/Gopeshwar.
3. **Acclimatization Protocols:** Our itinerary is strictly designed to allow optimal acclimatization to prevent AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness).
